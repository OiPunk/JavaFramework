# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation respectful, inclusive, and constructive for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and professional
- Give actionable, technical feedback
- Assume good intent and ask clarifying questions
- Focus on ideas, not people

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks
- Public or private intimidation
- Deliberate disruption of collaboration
- Sharing private information without permission

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this code.
Repeated violations may lead to temporary or permanent ban from participation.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness misconduct, open a private issue with maintainers and include:

- What happened
- Where it happened
- Evidence (if available)

Reports will be handled as confidentially as possible.
